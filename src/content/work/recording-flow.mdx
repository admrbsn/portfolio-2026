## The problem with a countdown

Every version of this flow had opened with a three-second countdown. It felt
considerate. In testing it turned out to be the opposite — three seconds is
exactly long enough to start second-guessing yourself.

<PullQuote>
  The countdown was not preparing people. It was giving them time to panic.
</PullQuote>
